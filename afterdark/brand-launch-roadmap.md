# Brand launch roadmap (Origins Program, $0 to $100k/mo)

Order agreed with Daniel on 2026-10-26, adapted from the Origins Program course. Numbers in parentheses are course lesson numbers in the Origins Program Source Library.

## Product and numbers
1. Find a product (15 to 18)
2. Set up your tools and supplier (13, 14)
3. Know your numbers: margin, break-even ROAS, target ROAS and minimum daily spend (20, 82, 84)

## Research
4. Understand your product (27 to 30)
5. Find the market's desires (31 to 34)
6. Work out market sophistication (35)
7. Find new mechanisms and new information (36 to 39)

## Avatars
8. Build core avatars, including new identity (40 to 44)
9. Build sub avatars (45, 46)

## Offer
10. Plan and create your offer (19, 21 to 26)

## Creative planning
11. Come up with ad angles (48 to 50)
12. Choose a testing method and plan your first 5 to 10 concepts (51 to 54)
13. Add the concepts to the creative roadmap (55, 56)

## Making ads
14. Make image ads (57 to 60)
15. Study winning video ads (61, 62)
16. Write video ads (63 to 66)
17. Get content and edit the videos (67 to 69)

## Launch and scale
18. Set up Facebook assets (70 to 77)
19. Launch and test your ads and offer (78 to 81)
20. Scale up and down (83, 85)

## Learnings
21. Review losing and winning ads (86, 88)
22. Turn what you learned into new ads (87, 89)
23. Complete the feedback loop and repeat (90)

## Changes from the course order
- Target ROAS and minimum daily spend moved into step 3 because they come from the same numbers as break-even ROAS.
- The offer moved after research and avatars, and before ad angles. Lesson 19 says offers are tested inside Meta once ads run.
- Market sophistication comes first, then mechanisms and new information in one research pass. New identity is handled inside the avatar work (lessons 35 and 40).
- Images come before video (lesson 53, 09:46).
