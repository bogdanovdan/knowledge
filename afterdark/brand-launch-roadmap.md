# Brand launch roadmap (Origins Program, $0 to $100k/mo)

Order agreed with Daniel on 2026-10-26, adapted from the Origins Program course. Numbers in parentheses are course lesson numbers in the Origins Program Source Library. "EM modules" are modules of the Email Marketing (retention) course library, added on 2026-10-26 because Origins does not cover retention. "BS" in brackets marks where the Blue Sense Digital video library (328 YouTube transcripts) complements a step. It adds no new steps.

## Product and numbers
1. Find a product (15 to 18) [BS: little]
2. Set up your tools and supplier (13, 14) [BS: Shopify setup, operations, fulfillment and 3PL]
3. Know your numbers: margin, break-even ROAS, target ROAS and minimum daily spend (20, 82, 84) [BS: finance, P&L, cash flow and pricing]

## Research
4. Understand your product (27 to 30) [BS: little]
5. Find the market's desires (31 to 34)
6. Work out market sophistication (35)
7. Find new mechanisms and new information (36 to 39)

## Avatars
8. Build core avatars, including new identity (40 to 44)
9. Build sub avatars (45, 46) [BS: little]

## Offer
10. Plan and create your offer (19, 21 to 26) [BS: offers, pricing, AOV, bundles and landing page CRO]

## Creative planning
11. Come up with ad angles (48 to 50) [BS: creative strategy]
12. Choose a testing method and plan your first 5 to 10 concepts (51 to 54) [BS: creative strategy]
13. Add the concepts to the creative roadmap (55, 56) [BS: creative strategy]

## Making ads
14. Make image ads (57 to 60) [BS: ad production]
15. Study winning video ads (61, 62) [BS: ad production]
16. Write video ads (63 to 66) [BS: ad production]
17. Get content and edit the videos (67 to 69) [BS: ad production]

## Launch and scale
18. Set up Facebook assets (70 to 77) [BS: Meta account structure, tracking, Google Ads and Merchant Center setup]
19. Set up email and SMS: sending domain, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, pop-up and capture forms (EM modules 1, 2, 12, 13 setup) [BS: retention]
20. Build the core flows: welcome, abandoned cart and post-purchase, with design and copy (EM modules 3, 4, 7, 11) [BS: retention]
21. Launch and test your ads and offer (78 to 81) [BS: Meta, Google Ads and CRO]
22. Scale up and down (83, 85) [BS: Meta and Google scaling, attribution, incrementality, other channels and international expansion]

## Retention
23. Segment the list: behavior, engagement, RFM and best customers (EM module 6) [BS: retention, AOV cohorts]
24. Run campaigns and newsletters, starting with few sends and ramping up, plus seasonal and SMS (EM modules 5, 8, 13) [BS: retention]
25. Run re-engagement, VIP, referral and launches to the list (EM module 11) [BS: retention]
26. Measure and optimize retention: metrics, A/B tests (1,000+ people per test), cohorts and AI (EM modules 9, 10) [BS: analytics]

## Learnings
27. Review losing and winning ads (86, 88) [BS: account and creative analysis]
28. Turn what you learned into new ads (87, 89) [BS: creative strategy]
29. Complete the feedback loop and repeat (90) [BS: analysis and agency management]

## Changes from the course order
- Target ROAS and minimum daily spend moved into step 3 because they come from the same numbers as break-even ROAS.
- The offer moved after research and avatars, and before ad angles. Lesson 19 says offers are tested inside Meta once ads run.
- Market sophistication comes first, then mechanisms and new information in one research pass. New identity is handled inside the avatar work (lessons 35 and 40).
- Images come before video (lesson 53, 09:46).
- Retention added from the Email Marketing course (2026-10-26). Email and SMS setup and the core flows go before launch so the first visitors are captured. The rest of retention runs after scaling, once there is a list. Neither course gives this order. It was agreed with Daniel.
- Blue Sense Digital content complements existing steps and adds none (2026-10-26). Former gaps were folded in: Google Ads into steps 18, 21 and 22, P&L and cash flow into step 3, attribution into step 22, CRO into steps 10 and 21. Supply chain and inventory remain thin.
