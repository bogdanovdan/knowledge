## [00:09:20] Applying the Marksman Method to Video Ads

[frame 00:09:20](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1a2-Vfjd4d4b1Odh7VL0n_q0PVlfMvJGm/view?usp=drivesdk) · `052_00-09-20.png`

*document*

**On screen:**

"How does the Marksman Method work with videos?"
For those of you who know, whenever we test the 3:2:2 method, the "3" stands for 3 variations of the HOOK, and potentially the bridge, but mainly just the first 3-10 seconds.
Then the HOLD, everything else after the first 3-10 seconds, is where we keep things the same.
"But if you're taking bigger swings and testing multiple angles, how do you connect all of those to the same hold?"
Great question
As long as the HOLD is universal enough to support all the angles your hooks set up... You can run the Marksman Method on videos, too.
Put in terms of this avatar training:
As long as your hold is relevant to the CORE AVATAR that you are targeting, then you are fine.
When you're testing the Marksman method with video, focus on making the hooks MORE specific and the holds more generic.
Example From Above:
• Hook 1: "POV: You work night shift"
• Hook 2: "POV: You're a light sleeper"
• Hook 3: "POV: You have insomnia"
