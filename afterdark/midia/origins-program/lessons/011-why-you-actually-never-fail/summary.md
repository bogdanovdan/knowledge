## Overview
This lesson addresses entrepreneurial mindset and reframes the concept of failure for students building an e-commerce business. Using an interview clip featuring billionaire Michael Rubin, the instructor demonstrates why setbacks like losing ad campaigns or flopped product launches are merely feedback steps toward success. The material is aimed at beginners and operators navigating early campaign tests and operational setbacks.

* Michael Rubin's blueprint for starting over includes three core actions: studying non-stop, finding a void in the market, sprinting at that void, and surrounding oneself with the right people while asking for help [00:01:01].

* The instructor highlights that successful entrepreneurs are willing to endure failure and negative outcomes longer than average people [00:04:02].

## Frameworks and models
### Michael Rubin's Rebuild Framework
As stated in the featured interview [00:01:01]:
1. **Continuous study:** Be a non-stop studier.
2. **Identify opportunity:** Locate an open void in the market.
3. **Execution sprint:** Sprint directly at the identified void immediately.
4. **Team & assistance:** Assemble the right team and never hesitate to ask for help.

### The E-commerce Feedback Loop
The instructor outlines how to process operational failures [00:04:14]:
1. Run test (ad, landing page, offer, or product).
2. Encounter negative result / flop.
3. Complete the feedback loop: analyze the outcome and determine the underlying reason for the result.
4. Adjust course and execute the next iteration one step closer to success.
