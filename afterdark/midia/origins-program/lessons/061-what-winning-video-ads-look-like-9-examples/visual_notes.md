## [00:02:15] Ad Strategy Analysis - Selling With Negative Curiosity

[frame 00:02:15](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1x5_gHJRx607MDQiO1wgZglHRzyjxrEUz/view?usp=drivesdk) · `061_00-02-15.png`

*slide*

**On screen:**

AI VOICEOVER
Selling With Negative Curiosity
MOST AWARE
Why This Ad Is Great :
#1 - Pattern Interrupt:
Meta feeds are flooded with overly positive or salesy openings. A negative or "angry" line breaks the pattern and feels real or authentic.
#2 - Curiosity Gap:
The viewer's brain asks: "Wait, why does this person want their money back?" That unanswered tension keeps them hooked until the reveal.
#3 - Authenticity & Relatability:
Viewers trust people who seem skeptical or frustrated - it mirrors how they feel toward ads. By starting "against" the product, it feels like a genuine review rather than an ad.

Ad headline: "Coffee Got An Upgrade! ☕
🍎 No Brain Fog
