## Overview
This lesson introduces a curated library of nine validated, high-performing video ads and demonstrates how to study them to improve ad writing skills. Aimed at marketers and brand owners scaling toward $100k/month who lack proven winners in their ad accounts, it focuses on analyzing ad angles, market awareness levels, and full-funnel elements rather than superficial creative choices.

## Key teachings
- Studying proven, winning ads is the fastest way to understand how to write high-converting video ads [00:00:14].
- Marketers stuck spending $100 to $200 per day at break-even often do not know what an actual scaling winning ad looks like [00:00:46].
- Analysis must go deeper than surface-level observations like emoji usage or specific hook lines; marketers must extract core strategic mechanisms and underlying buyer desires [00:00:59].
- High-level funnel analysis requires clicking through the ad to examine comment sections, social proof, and landing page advertorial copy [00:01:27].

## Frameworks and models

### Competitive Ad Research Workflow (AdSpy)
The instructor outlines a specific process for identifying true winning ads:
1. Search the target brand inside AdSpy.
2. Filter for ads active during the last 6 months.
3. Sort results by total share count (the ads with the most shares are 9 out of 10 times their top-performing ads).

### Ad Angle and Awareness Classifications
The presentation categorizes winning ads across specific strategic angles matched to awareness levels:
- **Using Speed to Sell** (Product Aware / Solution Aware): Focusing on rapid results (e.g., dental powder cleaning dog teeth fast) [00:00:25, 00:02:51].
- **Using Ease to Sell** (Problem Aware): Highlighting effortless implementation [00:02:00].
- **Leading With New Mechanism** (Product Aware): Introducing a novel method or product form factor to solve an old problem [00:02:10].
- **Selling With Negative Curiosity** (Most Aware / Product Aware): Opening with an angry or counter-intuitive statement to disrupt feeds and build curiosity [00:02:15, 00:03:00].
- **5 Reasons For Desire Visual** (Product Aware): Pairing multi-benefit lists with visual cues tapping into avatar desires [00:02:19].
- **Using Authority to Shift Beliefs** (Problem Aware): Leveraging credible figures (e.g., podcast/expert clips) to dismantle false beliefs [00:02:35].
- **Specific Avatar Pain Point** (Problem Aware): Targeting a hyper-specific sensory or situational problem (e.g., cold feet for hikers/athletes) [00:03:05].
- **Stopped Common Mechanism** (Product Aware): Explaining why the creator ceased using standard market solutions [00:03:35].
