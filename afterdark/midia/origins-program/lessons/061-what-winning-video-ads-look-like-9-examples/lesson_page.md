## Lesson page text

If you want to get better at making ads, you need to study what winning ads look like.

This is the fastest way for you to better understand how to write winning video ads.

The tools that I use to find winning video ads:

[https://app.adspy.com/](https://app.adspy.com/)

This is NOT an affilaite link.

I use AdSpy to search up brands, filter by "ads active during the last 6 months" and then I sort by shares. The ads with the most shares 9/10 are their top performing ads.
