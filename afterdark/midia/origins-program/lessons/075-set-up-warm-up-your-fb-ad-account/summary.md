## Overview
This lesson outlines how to create and warm up a new Meta ad account and Facebook Page to prevent ad account bans prior to running conversion ads. The instructor walks through account configuration, business payment setup, and launching a low-budget Page likes campaign with duplicated ads to build account trust. This content is aimed at e-commerce media buyers and entrepreneurs preparing to launch Meta paid acquisition campaigns.

## Key teachings
- Set the ad account time zone to the market you plan to sell to (such as US PST/Los Angeles) rather than your local time zone, because you cannot change time zone or currency after account creation [00:00:18].
- Match the ad account billing currency to your bank account's currency to avoid Meta's hidden 1% to 2% foreign currency transaction fee [00:00:51].
- Add payment methods directly inside Meta Business Manager (`Billing & payments`) first, rather than entering card details inside the ad account, to lower ban risk [00:01:54].
- Ensure the country, region, and billing address on the credit card match the details entered in Meta Business Manager and the ad account, as address mismatches can trigger account bans [00:03:34].
- Do not launch a Sales (conversion) campaign immediately on a new ad account; launching straight to conversions carries a high ban risk [00:04:35].
- Warm up the ad account and page using a Manual Engagement campaign set to Page likes [00:04:30], [00:05:13].
- Target "Worldwide" (including low-cost regions like India) for the warmup campaign to generate cheap impressions and follower volume rapidly [00:05:43], [00:06:34].
- Creative content for the warmup ad should simply be a neutral nature landscape photo paired with an inspirational quote to ensure guaranteed, problem-free ad approval [00:07:05].
- Building a history of approved ads signals account trust to Meta; reaching 50 approved ads ensures future sales campaigns get approved without issue [00:09:39].

## Frameworks and models
### Step-by-Step Ad Account Warmup Process
1. **Ad Account Setup:** Create the ad account in Business Manager using the target customer market's time zone and your billing card's primary currency [00:00:13].
2. **Business Manager Billing:** Navigate to `Billing & payments` in Business Manager and add your credit card. Assign this Business payment method as default within the ad account [00:02:10], [00:03:52].
3. **Warmup Campaign Creation:**
   - Objective: Engagement (`Manual engagement campaign`) [00:04:30].
   - Budget: $5/day Advantage campaign budget [00:04:47].
   - Conversion location: Facebook Page / Page likes [00:05:13].
   - Targeting: Worldwide [00:05:43].
4. **Initial Ad Launch:** Upload 1 ad consisting of a nature landscape image and a generic quote [00:07:18].
5. **The Duplication Stacking Method:**
   - Wait for the initial ad to finish `Processing` and transition to `Active` [00:08:50], [00:09:20].
   - Duplicate the active ad 4 times (yielding 5 total active ads) and publish [00:09:49].
   - Successively double the approved ads (5 to 10, 10 to 20, 20 to 40, up to 50) as each batch gets approved [00:10:07].
6. **Transition to Sales:** Run the campaign for 3 days ($15 total spend) until 50 ads are approved before publishing any Sales campaigns [00:10:44], [00:11:15].
