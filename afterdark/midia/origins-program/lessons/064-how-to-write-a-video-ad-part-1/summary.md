## Overview
This lesson demonstrates how to use Claude and a specialized 38-page prompt to script direct-response video ads for e-commerce products. Aimed at media buyers and brand founders who need scalable video creatives without filming custom footage, the lesson covers analyzing past ad performance, feeding customer research and mechanism data into Claude, and structuring the output into a modular video brief.

## Key teachings
- Continuously conduct customer research even after establishing creative direction; new iterations require deeper data rather than surface-level claims [00:00:38].
- Profitable sales on an ad do not make it a winner if it cannot spend; low ad spend and high frequency indicate retargeting traffic rather than scalable cold acquisition [00:01:25], [00:02:20].
- When an angle gets clicks but fails to scale due to low conversion or high skepticism, transition the concept from a static image to an educational video to explain the mechanism and build trust [00:03:36], [00:04:06].
- Prioritize "showing" rather than "telling" in ad copy (e.g., describing skipping lunch without hunger instead of stating "eating less is easy") [00:05:18].
- Always complete a performance feedback loop before scripting: feed the AI tool previous winners, losers, click-through rates, and hypotheses so it understands what failed [00:06:45], [00:09:20].
- Claude produces scripts that are roughly 70% to 80% finished; human intervention is mandatory to remove robotic phrasings, sharpen generic objections, and inject verbatim customer language [00:11:23], [00:25:08].
- Never fabricate compliance-sensitive claims; rely on truthful customer numbers or pivot to clinical trial participant data [00:12:40], [00:13:00].
- Build video tests with modular variations: test 3 distinct hooks paired with 3 congruent bridges, followed by a single universal hold and call to action [00:16:34], [00:18:40].
- Do not spend time searching for b-roll or visuals until the written script is properly structured and refined [00:22:09].

## Frameworks and models

### Ad Classification Framework
- **Losing Ad:** An ad turned off after 7 days or less, due to receiving less than 10% of total spend or underperforming against KPI benchmarks [00:02:00].
- **Winning Ad:** An ad that attracts more than 10% of total ad spend while hitting the target KPI [00:02:00].
- **Superwinning Ad:** An ad that allows the account budget to be scaled profitably at the target KPI [00:02:00].

### Pre-Script AI Feedback Loop
Before generating ad copy, the instructor supplies Claude with:
1. **Previous Performance Data:** Prior ad formats, winning hooks vs. losing hooks, spend share, and click-through metrics [00:07:05].
2. **Angle & Avatar Details:** Core desire, target demographic, and psychological triggers [00:10:05].
3. **Strategic Hypothesis:** The specific reason this creative iteration will succeed where earlier versions failed [00:10:05], [00:11:06].
4. **Foundational Documents:** Mechanism documentation, deep market research ("New Information"), and product specifications [00:10:13], [00:11:43].

### Modular Video Ad Architecture
A video structure totaling approximately 43–45 seconds:
- **Hook (0–3s):** Grabs attention using subtext, short phrasing (~5 words), and novel intrigue [00:06:10], [00:13:56].
  - *Hook A:* Science Discovery (e.g., age-specific biological shift) [00:13:56]
  - *Hook B:* Contrarian Problem (e.g., highlighting extreme diet friction) [00:15:15]
  - *Hook C:* Validation/Empathy (e.g., removing personal fault) [00:15:26]
- **Bridge (3–8s):** Connects the hook directly into the physiological enemy/mechanism (e.g., DPP-4 breaking down fullness signals) to make the ads distinct to Meta's delivery algorithm [00:15:35], [00:16:45].
- **Universal Hold (8–35s):** Shared across all 3 hook variations, following a 5-part progression:
  1. *Objection:* Addresses past failures directly [00:17:23].
  2. *Claim:* Introduces the proprietary mechanism/ingredient [00:17:28].
  3. *Proof:* Clinical trial statistics and social proof [00:17:34].
  4. *Benefit:* Behavioral scenarios using "Show, Don't Tell" [00:17:57].
  5. *Secondary Benefit:* Complementary features (e.g., skin/joint support) [00:18:08].
- **Universal Call to Action (35–45s):** Risk reversal guarantee, pricing breakdown, and direct click prompt [00:18:11].

## Numbers, benchmarks and rules of thumb
- **Spend threshold for winning ads:** A scalable creative must take more than 10% of total account ad spend [00:02:00].
- **Losing ad spending indicator:** Image ad captured under 5% of ad spend with CTR below 1% [00:01:30], [00:02:41].
- **Ad testing review window:** Review and turn off sub-KPI ads within 7 days or less [00:02:00].
- **Video ad duration target:** Target a maximum length of 43 to 45 seconds [00:18:49].
- **Hook and bridge length:** The hook runs 0–3 seconds; the hook combined with the bridge extends the distinct variation to roughly 8 seconds [00:16:05], [00:16:34].
- **AI script completion ratio:** Expect AI generation to provide 70% to 80% of the completed script; the remaining 20% to 30% requires human research and refinement [00:11:23], [00:25:08].
