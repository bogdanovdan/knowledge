## [00:05:50] What Is Market Sophistication Diagram and Levels

frame: 016_00-05-50.png (Drive: https://drive.google.com/file/d/195x196ecrbj1ImrPCRAX6nySzRDobOLb/view?usp=drivesdk)

*diagram*

**On screen:**

What Is Market Sophistication
Understanding how many products & solutions have been presented to that level of market awareness before
Market sophistication is the level of knowledge your market has on your products/services.
How many similar products has your market been told about before?
And how many similar products have they tried?

Chart:
Y-axis: Level of Market Sophistication
X-axis: Time, Amount and Quality of Buying Experiences
Trend arrow pointing up-right: Increasing Skepticism

Levels:
- Level 1: Brand New Market - Make any claim
- Level 2: Make a Bigger Claim than your Competitor
- Level 3: Introduce a Big Differentiator that supports the Claim and Convinces the Mind
- Level 4: Create a newer or better Differentiator, or improve on the previous one. "Widget 2.0"
- Level 5: Differentiator burns out. Audience skepticism is at an all time high. Full-on Empathic Marketing is now the only option.
[Attributed note in small font at bottom right]: Adapted from Eugene Schwartz, Breakthrough Advertising
