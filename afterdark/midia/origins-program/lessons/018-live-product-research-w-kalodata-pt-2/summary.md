## Overview
The lesson demonstrates live product research using Kalodata alongside Meta Ad Library, Google Trends, Reddit, and Similarweb to find and validate dropshipping products gaining traction on TikTok Shop. It walks through how to screen candidates, investigate trademarks, estimate competitor revenues, and research customer sentiment to uncover differentiated angles. The training is aimed at e-commerce beginners and intermediate dropshippers looking for a repeatable workflow to launch winning products without relying solely on copied creatives.

## Frameworks and models
### The 11-Step Kalodata Research & Validation Process
1. **Tool Access:** Access Kalodata (directly or via discounted Toolsuite) [00:00:23].
2. **Apply Initial Filters:** Navigate to TikTok USA Products; filter by "Last 7 Days", average unit price greater than $15, and high revenue growth [00:01:04].
3. **Screen Out Major Brands:** Eliminate listings owned by established household brands (e.g., Shark, Goli, Comfrt) [00:01:21].
4. **Brand Footprint Check:** Google the brand, checking follower counts across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube to confirm whether it is an untouchable enterprise or a small dropshipping operation [00:05:09], [00:07:51].
5. **Trademark Clearance:** Query the brand and product names in the USPTO database or ask Claude/ChatGPT. Rebrand the offer completely if trademarks exist [00:04:34], [00:33:08].
6. **Estimate Website Revenue:** Check the competitor's website on Similarweb. Use a baseline 1% conversion rate and the estimated average order value (AOV) to calculate estimated store revenue [00:09:56], [00:14:40].
7. **Social Listening & Market Sentiment:** Read top TikTok Shop video comments and search Reddit discussions (`[product/brand] site:reddit.com`) to evaluate product flaws, safety concerns, and customer expectations [00:16:00], [00:18:27].
8. **Competitor Ad Spy:** Check Meta Ad Library (and Atria) using generic product keywords to analyze active creative hooks, formats, and advertorials [00:11:43], [00:25:58].
9. **Angle & Mechanism Differentiation:** Identify either a new mechanism (e.g., highlighting an unaddressed feature, comparing against an expensive competitor) or an underserved avatar (e.g., targeting women for car tools, targeting tattooed customers for fleece leggings) [00:12:04], [00:39:50], [00:47:34].
10. **Supplier Margin Check:** Check AliExpress and request private supplier quotes. Ensure the target retail price represents approximately 3x the landed cost of goods sold (COGS + shipping) and aligns with market averages on Claude or Amazon [00:42:44], [00:46:14].
11. **Creative Compilation & Launch:** Download the top 10–20 best-performing TikTok creatives (with clean audio/no brand mentions), scrub the metadata, produce new differentiated angle concepts, and launch them together [00:58:15], [00:59:36].
