## Overview
This lesson demonstrates an end-to-end workflow for writing and building static image ads from customer research and master brand documents. The instructor illustrates how to use Claude and ChatGPT to generate angle variations, refine headline copy, extract benefit bullets, and assemble production creatives directly inside Canva. This training is aimed at direct-to-consumer brand founders and media buyers who need to launch fast testing batches without relying on professional graphic designers.

## Key teachings
- Writing copy for image ads and video ads follows the identical core research and angle generation process; video ads simply require more production time and execution formatting [00:00:04].
- A Concept is the overarching test idea (format, iteration, or avatar test), whereas an Angle is the specific mechanism or positioning strategy used to sell the product to the customer [00:01:05].
- High-performing hooks often pair two ideas that don't traditionally belong together (such as asserting that a difficult task is easy), which instantly triggers customer curiosity [00:04:51].
- When feeding prompts into AI tools like Claude, provide verbatim customer research quotes and avatar profiles; generic prompts yield generic copy [00:19:56], [00:24:00].
- When writing feature and benefit callout bullets, ensure claims remain realistic and outcome-focused rather than exaggerated miracle claims that trigger disbelief [00:19:10], [00:21:01].
- Sentence case or lowercase body text is preferable over all-caps for descriptive bullet points because it improves readability [00:21:23].
- Visual perfection is secondary to message strength in early testing; "ugly" ads with strong messaging, root-cause angles, and clear mechanisms frequently outperform heavily polished creatives [00:19:32], [00:22:07], [00:27:49].
- Product images with unwanted background text or badges can be rapidly cleaned by prompting ChatGPT to remove the text while preserving the original subject [00:15:25].

## Frameworks and models

### The Static Creative Production Workflow
The instructor executes a multi-step sequence to produce an image ad batch:
1. **Master Document Audit**: Pull core product mechanisms, avatar desires, emotional drivers, and verbatim customer quotes from the brand repository [00:00:27].
2. **Angle Generation (AI Collaboration)**: Feed avatar profiles and desired outcomes into Claude to generate 3 distinct angles with headline hooks [00:01:00].
3. **Fact and Baseline Checking**: Query ChatGPT for market benchmarks (e.g., average daily calorie intake for target demographic) to validate hook plausibility [00:04:14].
4. **Tracking and Creative Brief**: Populate the testing sheet and creative brief template with concepts, angles, testing methods (Marksman vs. Sniper), and headline variations [00:04:10], [00:12:30].
5. **Visual Template Selection**: Choose an existing high-converting static structure (e.g., the "Solution Exaggeration" layout) [00:12:50], [00:14:40].
6. **Asset Preparation & Text Cleansing**: Extract clean product or lifestyle imagery, using AI text removal and Canva background removal tools [00:15:25], [00:16:00].
7. **Bullet Point Generation**: Prompt Claude using exact structural verbs (`Reduces`, `Improves`, `Enhances`, `Provides`) mapped to customer pain quotes [00:18:20], [00:20:20].
8. **Assembly and Brand Alignment**: Match background colors and fonts to brand guidelines using an eyedropper tool, paste copy, and export single pages for testing [00:16:22], [00:27:07].

### Testing Approaches: Marksman vs. Sniper
- **Marksman Approach**: Testing multiple distinct angles or sub-avatars simultaneously within one batch to discover the best broad positioning [00:01:51], [00:12:23].
- **Sniper Approach**: Isolating a single, highly resonant angle and testing multiple headline or format variations exclusively around that angle [00:05:56], [00:08:27].

### Solution Exaggeration Template Breakdown
- **Headline**: Bold number or counter-intuitive claim to stop the scroll [00:12:50].
- **4 Feature/Benefit Pointers**: Four structured lines connected via pointer rules directly to the visual subject:
  - *Reduces* [Pain point / stress trigger]
  - *Improves* [Physiological response / biological function]
  - *Enhances* [Emotional control / desired state]
  - *Provides* [Lasting outcome / sensory relief] [00:20:20], [00:22:25].
- **Product Subject**: A clear isolated product image or in-use product context (e.g., powder scoop pouring into coffee) [00:15:20], [00:22:25].
