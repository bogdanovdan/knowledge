**Where To Get Content:**

[**Gridbank**](https://gridbank.io/) --> $250/month paid quarterly + fastest method to get any generic clip

[**Insense**](https://insense.pro/)--> $500/month paid quarterly + (UGC method 100% authentic + potential brand ambassador for filming in the future) **--> Save $100 with EASYA100**

[**Arcads**](https://www.arcads.ai/) --> $99/month paid monthly + this is something I don't really endorse. but my perspective on it is changing, and if you are using it for B-roll purposes, then you're fine if you ask me.

**Film/Source Yourself**--> This one cost $0. You can simply film the visuals yourself or you can hunt down creators on IG or. TikTok, get in contact, and ask them to film clips/videos for you.

**Source yourself** --> You can source from the internet from content creators with less than 5k followers. DO NOT RIP the video; you simply "rip" their clips. That said, this has copyright issues. Creators will message you asking for payment and compensation to use their content if they see your ad, and you can get banned. I was hesitant to add this in here, but this is something that people do. If you ask me, it's better than ripping, but still not that great.
