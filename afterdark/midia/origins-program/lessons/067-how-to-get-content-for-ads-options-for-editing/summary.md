## Overview
This lesson outlines practical pathways for sourcing and editing video ad creative, aimed at early-stage e-commerce store owners doing under $20k–$30k/month. The instructor covers specific platforms for obtaining footage, strategies for working with creators versus self-sourcing, copyright risks, and why founders should learn direct video editing before hiring outside talent.

## Key teachings
- Founders doing under $20k to $30k per month should learn to edit their own video ads because poor execution is a primary reason ads fail, and early-stage stores cannot attract or afford high-quality video editors [00:01:21], [00:13:02].
- Learning editing allows founders to brute-force early growth: the instructor cites a founder who reached $1M/month ($250k/month net profit) with just himself and a VA by editing and writing 2–3 ad concepts daily [00:00:38].
- The primary video formats to build are **Voiceover with B-roll** and **Subtitles with B-roll**; talking directly to the camera is rarely needed to produce a winning creative [00:05:52], [00:06:29].
- Writing a full script and waiting for an external UGC creator to film it slows down testing cycles excessively; creative tests should be executed quickly using existing footage before hiring creators for winning concepts [00:04:41], [00:05:26].
- AI avatars (such as Arcads) should not be used as talking heads claiming they tested or loved a physical product, but they can be used for B-roll or pointing elements [00:06:56], [00:07:19].
- Taking clips from creators with fewer than 5,000 followers without permission ("ethical ripping") presents serious copyright and account ban risks; creators frequently discover the ads and demand retroactive payouts [00:10:07], [00:11:33].
- When using sourced internet clips, founders must never include the creator's face and should only splice small segments into larger edits [00:11:06], [00:11:18].

## Frameworks and models

### Organic Creator Sourcing Process (from visual SOP)
A step-by-step outreach flow for finding UGC talent organically [00:07:55]:
- **Step 1:** Post on Twitter using UGC hashtags.
- **Step 2:** Search Collabstr requests.
- **Step 3:** Use TikTok search to locate creator profiles.
- **Step 4:** Send initial outreach emails.
- **Step 5:** Follow on Twitter and tag them in the original Twitter post (if applicable).
- **Step 6:** Follow and DM on Instagram; leave a comment on their latest post.
- **Step 7:** Send a LinkedIn connection (if applicable).
- **Step 8:** Follow up a total of 3 times via email/DM (one bump every 2 days across 4 days).

### Priority of Content Sourcing Methods
Ranked approach for obtaining video ad assets [00:12:24]:
1. **Film/Source Yourself** ($0, phone or direct outreach) or **Stock UGC platforms (Gridbank)**.

3. **AI B-roll generation tools**.
4. **Internet clip sourcing (<5k follower accounts)** — considered a high-risk last resort.
