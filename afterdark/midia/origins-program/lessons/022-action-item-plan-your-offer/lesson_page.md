## Lesson page text

#### **Time To Put Into Action**

Now that you have been given the information on how to know your numbers with specific offers and the different types of offers that you can run, I want you to go and start planning some offers that you could potentially build inside of the offer sheet linked here:

 My rule of thumb is to always try to keep your margins above 60%. Anytime you go below 60%, it can start to be a little bit slippery.

 Ideally, you want to keep margins at a bare minimum of 50%, and ideally closer to 70%.

(Which is why 60-70% is more of a sweet spot)

 That said, if you can put yourself in a position where you have 70% to 80% margins and you are seeing good conversions.

 Then by all means, you can also try to have more aggressive margins as well.
