## Overview
This lesson teaches e-commerce brand owners how to calculate product gross margins, establish a break-even ROAS, and set a target scaling ROAS for paid advertising. It also demonstrates how scaling spend can remain profitable even when ROAS drops, and outlines the proper time attribution windows for making scaling decisions without reacting to daily volatility.

## Key teachings
- Calculate gross profit margin strictly using product selling price, supplier/shipping costs, and processing fees; do not factor in fixed overhead like video editors, personal pay, software subscriptions, or Shopify platform costs [00:01:24].
- Overestimate costs (such as adding transaction fees or handling padding) rather than underestimating them to avoid believing an unprofitable operation is profitable [00:00:53].
- To establish your target scaling ROAS, take your break-even ROAS (rounded up) and add 1.0 to it [00:02:48].
- Lower ROAS multiples can be acceptable when scaling as long as the increase in ad spend yields higher net profit in absolute dollars [00:04:36].
- As Meta ad budgets scale up, ROAS naturally decreases because ads must reach broader, colder audiences [00:07:49].
- Scaling and ad budget management decisions must not be made off single-day fluctuations; evaluate performance over a 3-day minimum window or a 7-day window [00:05:42], [00:07:29].
- Significant data is required before altering budgets; do not scale up based on isolated results like a single purchase on an ad set [00:07:59].
- If an account is hovering around break-even ROAS, the priority should be creative testing (new ads) rather than scaling spend [00:09:04].

## Frameworks and models
**Target ROAS Calculation Process**
1. *Determine Product COGS:* Combine supplier price, inbound shipping, and a processing fee buffer (e.g., ~3% Shopify transaction fee) [00:00:20].
2. *Calculate Gross Margin & Break-even ROAS:* Plug price and costs into the margin calculator. Round the resulting break-even ROAS up [00:02:39].
3. *Set Target ROAS:* `Target ROAS = Break-even ROAS + 1.0` [00:02:57].
4. *Evaluate Scaling Profitability:* Test spend scenarios to verify that the absolute profit dollar figure increases even if the target ROAS dips [00:04:00].

## Numbers, benchmarks and rules of thumb
- **Shopify/Payment processing fee buffer:** ~3% (e.g., $1.50 on a $50 item) [00:00:37].
- **Target ROAS rule of thumb:** Break-even ROAS + 1.0 (e.g., 1.5 break-even results in a 2.5 target ROAS; 1.6 break-even results in a 2.6 target ROAS) [00:02:57].
- **Decision attribution windows:** 3 days bare minimum; 7 days recommended for accounts spending around $100/day [00:05:42], [00:07:29].
