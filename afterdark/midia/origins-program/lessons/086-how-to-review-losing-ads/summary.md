## Overview

This lesson teaches media buyers and e-commerce brand owners how to conduct a structured creative post-mortem on losing ad concepts inside Meta Ads Manager. The instructor demonstrates how to evaluate ad performance using percentage of spend rather than raw dollar figures, break down creative headlines and congruence, and turn qualitative observations into testable hypotheses. The session aims to help operators refine their copywriting and iterate losing concepts into potential winners rather than abandoning ideas prematurely.

## Key teachings

- Review losing ads by filtering Meta Ads Manager for the exact 7-day date window they ran, then sort variations by total spend to identify which creative Meta favored [00:00:20].
- When an ad receives only a tiny fraction of spend (e.g., ~2%), classify the entire concept as a "loser" rather than forcing budget onto underperforming variations by turning off the top spender [00:09:23], [00:10:53].
- Rely on percentage of total ad account spend over a 7-day evaluation window to assess ad traction across any budget tier [00:05:05], [00:07:41].
- Do not make broad, dismissive assumptions like "this image sucks"; diagnose messaging structure, pattern interrupts, and claim credibility instead [00:25:21].
- Strong scroll-stopping angles frequently contrast two elements that do not belong together (e.g., stating that maintaining a 1,200-calorie deficit is "easy") [00:14:16].
- Ad learnings are educated guesses, never established facts, until re-tested and validated by a resulting winning ad [00:26:15], [00:27:02].
- When stuck diagnosing why an ad failed, benchmark the creative against known high-performing static ad templates to spot missing ingredients (e.g., clear avatar call-outs, mechanism credibility, or specific numbers) [00:20:18].
- Ensure visual and conceptual congruence between ad claims, the product mechanism highlighted, and the destination landing page [00:18:49], [00:19:30].
- An action plan from a losing ad does not require immediate re-iteration; it can simply be an explicit decision not to repeat that specific creative approach [00:28:49], [00:29:39].

## Frameworks and models

### The Losing Ad Analysis Workflow
1. **Filter Date Range**: Set Meta Ads Manager date window to the specific 7-day period the batch ran [00:00:24].
2. **Sort by Spend**: Identify which specific variation received the highest percentage of ad spend [00:00:36], [00:11:59].
3. **Record Core Metrics**: Log Amount Spent, ROAS/CPA, CTR, CVR, AOV, Hook Rate, Hold Rate, and Percentage of Spend into a learning document or creative roadmap [00:01:40], [00:02:19].
4. **Compare Variations**: Evaluate why Meta preferred one variation over the others (headline phrasing, visual contrast, odd specificity, power frames) [00:12:07], [00:16:34].
5. **Benchmark Against Proven Frameworks**: Compare against established static templates (e.g., *Avatar Call Out*, *Solution Exaggeration*, *Stealing Credibility*, *This Is Not [The Product]*, *Specific Pain Point*) to spot missing elements [00:20:25]–[00:23:15].
6. **Formulate Educated Guesses**: Hypothesize what caused the drop-off (lack of mechanism explanation, disconnect with landing page offer, weak hook) [00:19:30], [00:26:20].
7. **Define the Action Plan**: Document whether to abandon the angle or iterate the concept into a better format (e.g., expanding into a video explanation) [00:23:41], [00:24:30].

### Custom Metric Formulas in Meta Ads Manager
- `CVR = Purchases / Link clicks` (formatted as a percentage) [00:02:46]
- `Hook Rate = 3-second video plays / Impressions` (formatted as a percentage) [00:03:38]
- `Hold Rate (All) = ThruPlays / Impressions` (formatted as a percentage) [00:03:51]
- `Hook-to-Hold Rate = ThruPlays / 3-second video plays` (percentage of 3-second viewers who reach 15 seconds) [00:04:18]

## Numbers, benchmarks and rules of thumb

- **Minimum run time**: Allow an ad concept to run for at least 7 days before making definitive cut or iteration decisions [00:08:24].
- **Creative output target**: Aim to produce 1 new ad creative per day (~7 ads per week) [00:08:35].
- **Active ad set limit**: Maintain a maximum of 5 to 7 ad sets running concurrently inside a $100 CBO to avoid overwhelming Meta's algorithm [00:08:50].
- **Percentage of account spend benchmarks (7-day window)**:
  - `< 10%` (e.g., ~2%): Loser; ad did not get traction or scale [00:07:05], [00:09:25].
  - `10%`: Threshold where sufficient data exists to evaluate performance [00:07:16].
  - `30% to 50%`: Indicates a likely winning ad concept [00:07:28].
- **Analysis time**: Spend 10 to 15 minutes processing learnings on a losing ad; spend 30 to 40 minutes analyzing a winning ad [00:25:47].
