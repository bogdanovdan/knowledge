## Frameworks and models
### 3:2:2 Campaign Structure [00:00:50 visual note]
- **Campaign level:** 1 overarching campaign.
- **Ad set level:**
  - 1 Champions Ad Set (housing proven winning ads).
  - Multiple Creative Test Ad Sets (recommended maximum of 5 active ad sets total when spending a few hundred dollars daily).
- **Ad level:** Each creative test ad set contains:
  - 3 creatives (built around the same concept)
  - 2 primary text copies
  - 2 headlines

### Target ROAS Calculation Formula [00:00:30 visual note]
`Target ROAS = Breakeven ROAS + 1`
*(Example: A breakeven ROAS of 1.6 gives a scaling target ROAS of 2.6).*
