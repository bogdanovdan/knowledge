## Overview
This lesson demonstrates how to construct core customer avatars by mapping product features and benefits into specific, actionable desires. Geared toward e-commerce and Shopify store owners scaling toward $100k/month, the instructor walks through live audience research across Meta Ad Library, Reddit, and Google AI/Claude. The session establishes how to select the right scope of desire for an early-stage brand and connect avatar definitions directly to ad hooks and headline angles.

## Key teachings
- For early-stage brands, begin avatar building from a core desire rather than demographics, as desire directly informs ad positioning and copywriting [00:03:35].
- Highly generic desires generated by AI tools (e.g., "freedom in living situation," "wants solutions that work") should be discarded; focus instead on functional, high-intent desires rooted directly in what the product delivers [00:02:40], [00:08:44].
- Brands earn the right to target broader desires as revenue grows; brands under $100k/month should start narrow and specific [00:09:20], [00:19:25].
- Desires must be evaluated along three distinct dimensions: Scope, Urgency, and Staying Power [00:16:10].
- When entering a competitive space, research competitors' active ads in the Meta Ad Library to identify which specific angles and sub-avatars they are serving (e.g., nurseries, night-shift workers, light sleepers) [00:20:20], [00:22:26].
- Direct research on platforms like Reddit uncovers real customer vocabulary, routines, and objections (such as sleep tracker metrics, magnesium usage, and fasting habits) that can be turned directly into ad angles [00:27:15], [00:34:25].
- Using "New Information" as an ad mechanism enables brands to hook an avatar by introducing a surprising fact (e.g., how nighttime snacks or light exposure affect sleep cycles) before presenting the product as the solution [00:29:20], [00:43:25].
- AI assistants like Claude or ChatGPT should be used as iterative brainstorming tools to turn raw audience research notes into candidate headlines [00:38:08], [00:39:40].

## Frameworks and models

### Avatar Scale Framework
A spectrum used to visualize the specificity of a customer avatar [00:08:55]:
- **One Pepe (Narrow end):** Highly specific profile (e.g., *"34-year-old dentist in Tampa who drives a red Tesla and drinks oat milk lattes"*). Small audience, high relatability.
- **All Pepes (Broad end):** Maximally broad definition (e.g., *"You have a heartbeat"* or *"Wants to enhance health"*). Massive audience, high competition, lower inherent urgency.

### Core Avatar Selection by Revenue Stage
Guidelines for choosing core avatar breadth based on monthly revenue [00:01:15], [00:19:25]:
1. **Stage 1 ($0–$100k/month):** Choose a single core avatar focused on a narrow, specific desire (or experience/behavior). Build targeted sub-avatars beneath it. "Out-avatar" competitors rather than out-marketing them. Test for 2–4 weeks before considering a shift to a new core desire.
2. **Stage 2 ($100k–$1M/month):** Move into mid-tier desires (e.g., *"Wants restorative sleep"*).
3. **Stage 3 ($1M–$10M/month):** Scale into top-level, broad desires (e.g., *"Wants to enhance overall health"*).

### 3 Dimensions of Desire Evaluation
Used to assess the commercial strength of potential desires [00:16:10]:
1. **Scope:** How many people share the desire?
2. **Urgency:** How desperately do they want or need immediate relief?
3. **Staying Power:** Does the desire renew continuously (like hunger) or resolve permanently once satisfied?

*Instinct power ranking:*

- `#2` Status (Amplified by social media)
- `#3` Sex (Fundamental, though platform-restricted)
- `#4` Comfort (Elevated by modern consumer expectations)

### Avatar Construction Hierarchy
The document hierarchy demonstrated during the workshop [00:33:45], [00:44:15]:
- **Labels:** Descriptive tags used to identify the group (e.g., *"Light Sleeper"*).
- **Core Avatar (Desire-Based, Experience-Based, or Behavior-Based):** The foundational driver (e.g., *"Wants to improve their REM/Deep sleep"*).
- **Sub-Avatar:** A layered qualification incorporating a specific experience, behavior, or belief (e.g., *Product Experience: Has tried magnesium*).
