## Overview

This lesson introduces the Origins Creative Roadmap spreadsheet used to plan, track, and analyze advertising ideas from conception through performance evaluation [00:00:00]. The instructor explains the workflow statuses, how to connect concepts to specific customer desires and testing methods, and how to classify winning versus losing ads [00:00:24, [00:04:43]]. The lesson is aimed at media buyers and founders setting up a disciplined, structured ad ideation and testing pipeline [00:00:10, 00:07:42].

## Key teachings

- Ad concepts should only be entered into the main roadmap once they are thoughtfully planned; avoid cluttering the primary tracker with dozens of unprocessed brainstorms [00:00:15, 00:07:25].
- The "Done" status should never be selected until performance learnings are fully processed and documented in the sheet [00:00:44, 00:00:53].
- Every ad idea must tie back to a single validated customer desire identified during avatar research (e.g., "I want restorative sleep") [00:01:28, 00:01:42].
- For image tests using the Marksman method, test multiple angles simultaneously; for video tests using the Sniper method, focus on a single angle [00:01:57, 00:02:23].
- When documenting an ad rationale, sell yourself on why the test will work by articulating the reason, adding proof links (e.g., Reddit threads), and sketching hook concepts before writing the full brief [00:03:02, 00:03:34].
- Maintain a direct link to the published Facebook ad in the spreadsheet to make reviewing and writing learnings quick and accurate [00:04:31].
- The ultimate objective of ad testing is finding "super winning" ads that allow budget expansion at target KPIs, rather than small-spend ads that merely break even [00:06:24, 00:06:38].

## Frameworks and models

### Creative Roadmap Status Pipeline
1. **Working:** The ad concept is actively being created or written [00:00:28].
2. **Filming:** Optional status used only if an external UGC creator is recording content (generally not recommended by the instructor for beginners) [00:00:35].
3. **Learning:** The ad has been launched and is actively running in its test window [00:00:32].
4. **Done:** The ad has concluded testing AND complete post-campaign learnings have been entered in the spreadsheet [00:00:44, 00:00:57].

### Ad Performance Classifications
- **Losing Ad:** An ad that gets turned off after 7 days or less because it captures less than 10% of total account ad spend, or because performance falls below KPI [00:05:00, 00:05:15].
- **Winning Ad:** An ad that captures more than 10% of total account spend while meeting target KPI performance [00:05:54, 00:06:01].
- **Superwinning Ad (Advanced / Evolve level):** An ad that captures the majority of spend and allows budget scaling at target KPI (e.g., scaling account spend from $100/day to $300/day) [00:06:18, 00:06:31].

### Creative Roadmap Spreadsheet Structure
The sheet organizes creative data across the following core columns:
- **Status:** Workflow state (`Working`, `Learning`, `Filming`, `Done`) [00:00:28].
- **BATCH #:** Batch identifier for the creative run [00:00:45 visual notes].
- **Author:** Team member creating the concept [00:00:45 visual notes].
- **Ad Concept:** Creative execution type (e.g., `Direct Avatar Callout`) [00:01:24].
- **Mass Desire:** The target avatar desire being tapped [00:01:28].
- **Angle:** Specific messaging angles tested (e.g., Marksman tests use 3 angles; Sniper tests use 1 angle) [00:01:57, 00:02:23].
- **What are you creating / testing...:** The hypothesis, reason for confidence, proof links, and potential hooks [00:03:02, 00:04:00 visual notes].
- **Ad Format:** Simplified to `Static` or `Video` [00:04:01].
- **Link to Brief:** URL to the detailed creative brief [00:04:14].
- **Link to Ad:** Direct URL to the ad within Meta Ads Manager [00:04:31].
- **Results:** Classification (`Winning Ad` vs. `Losing Ad`) [00:04:37, 00:05:54].
- **Learnings:** Deep qualitative breakdown explaining why the customer cared or did not care [00:00:49, 00:06:55].

## Numbers, benchmarks and rules of thumb

- **Spend threshold for winning vs. losing ads:** An ad must capture at least 10% of total ad spend while hitting target KPI to qualify as a winner [00:05:07, 00:05:54].
- **Testing evaluation window:** 7 days or less before turning off an ad that fails to hit KPI or fails to capture spend [00:05:00, 00:05:15].
- **Spend example benchmark:** If account spend is $100/day ($700 over 7 days), an ad spending only $10/day ($70 total) is considered low spend and not scaling [00:05:24, 00:05:50].
- **Scale benchmark (Superwinner):** An ad that enables an account to scale spend from $100/day to $300/day at KPI while absorbing the majority of spend [00:06:24].
- **Idea management rule of thumb:** Keep 4 to 5 active, high-priority ad ideas in the main roadmap rather than dumping 50 unprocessed ideas into the sheet [00:07:25, 00:07:42].
- **Hit rate dashboard formula:** `Hit Rate % = SUM(Creative Roadmap!K2) / (Creative Roadmap!K2 + Creative Roadmap!L2)` [00:00:05 visual notes].
