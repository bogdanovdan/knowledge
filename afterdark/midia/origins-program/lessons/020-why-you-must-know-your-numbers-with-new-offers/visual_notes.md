## [00:03:40] Important Note #2: Knowing Margins with New Offers

frame: 020_00-03-40.png (Drive: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1V84kkCpmkM5kgWi0oI9VcYHQkQOdQQw5/view?usp=drivesdk)

*slide*

**On screen:**

⭐ IMPORTANT NOTE #2
Prior to setting up any offer ALWAYS use the profit calculator to establish the new targets for ROAS & CPA so you know whether or not it is successful

Evergreen Example:
If your ROAS is currently a 2x and your break even ROAS is 1.43x (70% Gross profit) and your spending $100k per month, and you're generating $200k total.
Your Gross Profit = $40,000 ($200k rev * 0.7 gp = $140k gp - $100k spend)

Sales Example:
But if you launch a 30% offer that takes you to a 2x ROAS but your breakeven is now 1.82x (55% Gross profit) and now your spending $150k per month, generating $300k total
Gross Profit = $15,000 ($300k rev * 0.55 gp = $165kgp - $150k spend)
