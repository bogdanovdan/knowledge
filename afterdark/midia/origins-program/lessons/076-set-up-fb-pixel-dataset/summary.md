## Overview
This lesson demonstrates how to set up and connect a Meta Pixel and Conversions API tracking on a Shopify store. It is aimed at beginners and store owners configuring their core advertising assets without relying on complex third-party tracking tools.

## Key teachings
- Use the official, free Shopify "Facebook & Instagram" sales channel app rather than third-party tracking solutions [00:00:16].
- Ensure your personal Facebook profile has admin access to your Meta Business Manager, Facebook page, and ad account before starting setup [00:00:42].
- Keep the Shopify data sharing preference set to "Maximum" so customer activity is tracked using Meta Pixel, advanced matching, and Conversions API [00:01:08].
- Creating or connecting the pixel via the Shopify app automatically provisions it within Meta Business Manager, eliminating the need to create it manually in Business Manager first [00:01:51].
- After completing the Shopify setup, verify in Meta Business Manager that the dataset/pixel is connected to the intended ad account, and connect it manually if it is not linked automatically [00:02:13].

## Frameworks and models
### Shopify Facebook & Instagram Setup Workflow
1. **Install App**: Download and install the free "Facebook & Instagram" app from the Shopify App Store [00:00:20].
2. **Connect Accounts**: Click **Get Started**, connect the Facebook account holding admin rights, and choose the correct Business Manager/portfolio [00:00:38].
3. **Data Sharing**:
   - Set preference to **Maximum** [00:01:08].
   - Select an existing pixel/dataset or click **Create new** and confirm [00:01:19].
4. **Advertising & Business Details**: Select the appropriate ad account, confirm contact details, accept the Seller Agreement terms, and submit [00:01:34].
5. **Verify Meta Business Suite Connection**:
   - Navigate to `business.facebook.com` > **Settings** > **Data Sources** > **Datasets & pixels** [00:02:04].
   - Select the dataset and open the **Connected assets** tab [00:02:18].
   - If the ad account is missing, click **Connect assets**, select the ad account, and confirm [00:02:22].
