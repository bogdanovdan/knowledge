- Usually, has background music flows with the ad
- Heavily edited with multiple clips

Slideshow Video w Text:
- Static images with transitions
- Heavy text overlay with movement
- Text reveals

Authentic UGC/Talking head only
- Person speaking directly to camera (can be real or AI - use ai ay your own risk)
- Casual, authentic feel
- Usually single-take or minimal editing
