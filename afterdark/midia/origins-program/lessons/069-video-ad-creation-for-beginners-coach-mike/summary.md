## Overview
Coach Mike presents an end-to-end beginner workshop on producing direct-to-consumer (DTC) video ads, breaking down his software stack, UGC creator sourcing methods, and video assembly workflow. The lesson covers script ideation with AI, voice cloning, competitor research, and step-by-step editing inside CapCut Desktop. It is aimed at early-stage e-commerce operators who want to transition from static ads to video creatives or learn to edit their own video assets without relying on expensive agencies.

## Frameworks and models
### The AI-Powered Creative Pipeline
1. **Research & Inspiration:** Monitor competitor libraries using spy tools (Atria) to identify high-performing formats and concepts [00:22:46].
2. **Script Generation:** Transcribe winning scripts and rewrite them inside an AI tool (Claude) loaded with customer review data, angles, and avatar research [00:26:07], [00:32:00].
3. **Voice & Avatar Production:** Generate voiceovers using cloned creator voices or stock/custom AI avatars (ElevenLabs, Arcads) [00:05:07], [00:15:09].
4. **Footage Gathering:** Source raw footage via creator gifting, stock video sites, or AI image-to-video generation [00:10:45], [00:18:45].
5. **Timeline Assembly:** Assemble the edit inside CapCut with standardized tracks (A-roll, B-roll, captions, background audio, SFX) [01:00:06].
6. **Hook Splitting & Export:** Produce Hook 1, Hook 2, and Hook 3 variations on the identical body edit and export separately [01:18:58].

### Systematic Ad Iteration Process
- **Step 1:** Launch 3 variations of a concept with identical body footage, identical CTA, but 3 distinct audio/spoken hooks [00:30:42], [00:44:55].
- **Step 2:** Isolate the winning hook based on ad spend and click distribution inside Meta [00:34:53], [00:45:14].
- **Step 3 (Audio Iteration):** Produce 3 new hook variations testing slight phrasing tweaks or audience callouts based on the winning audio [00:45:34].
- **Step 4 (Visual Iteration):** Retain the winning audio hook and body, but swap the actor, visual hook, or caption styling to determine whether retention was driven by the creator appearance or the spoken words [00:35:12], [00:46:41].

## Numbers, benchmarks and rules of thumb
- **Ad length:** 30 to 40 seconds (maximum 50 seconds) for standard DTC UGC ads [00:44:25].
- **Volume output:** 3 to 4 concepts per week, equaling 12 creative assets (3 hook variations per concept) [00:29:29], [00:42:42].
- **Scene pacing / Retention rule:** Introduce a visual change, cut, caption, or sound effect every 2 to 3 seconds; keep B-roll cuts under 1 to 2 seconds [01:13:02].
- **Influencer follower size target:** Micro-influencers with 1,000 to 2,000 followers for product seeding/gifting [00:18:54].
- **Audio mixing benchmark:** Keep spoken voice and primary sound around the -12 dB to -6 dB mark, lowering background music to ensure vocals are completely clear [01:01:50].
- **Caption typography settings:** Proxima Nova SemiBold font, white text, black stroke around 4 pixels, font size 14 (or CapCut size 40), 1 to 2 short lines maximum per frame [00:36:43], [00:58:20].
- **Testing budget:** £100 per ad set when testing in an ABO [01:26:47].
