## Overview

This lesson covers the manual and AI-assisted process of "desire hunting"—scouring Reddit threads, customer reviews, YouTube videos, and AI tools to uncover the core mass desires, frustrations, and exact vocabulary of potential customers [00:00:00]. It demonstrates how to translate raw customer feedback into advertising angles, hooks, and proof mechanisms for physical products, using a collagen brand as a primary case study [00:01:27]. This training is aimed at direct-to-consumer e-commerce brand owners and media buyers seeking to build compliant, high-converting creatives grounded in market research rather than guesswork [00:00:16].

## Key teachings

- **Manual research cannot be fully skipped for AI:** Golden nuggets, exact vocabulary, and novel hooks reside deep in Reddit comment threads, YouTube comments, and review sections; relying solely on AI causes marketers to miss nuance and prevents the necessary internal understanding of the customer [00:01:01], [00:27:49].
- **Use exact customer phrasing over marketer assumptions:** Marketers often project words like "hydrated-looking skin," but actual customer comments use phrases like "smoother and less dry skin"; ad copy should use the customer’s precise phrasing [00:04:42].
- **Create proxy proof metrics for invisible products:** Supplements like collagen cannot visually prove efficacy instantly; copywriters must identify relatable proxy indicators that customers track (e.g., trimming nails once a week instead of every three weeks) to prove a supplement works or highlight that a competitor's product is weak [00:06:08], [00:08:22].
- **Speed is a primary conversion driver:** Rapid transformation timelines (e.g., results in days or weeks) are powerful selling propositions in skincare and wellness; however, clinical realities and consumer beliefs must guide testing [00:15:58], [00:16:24].
- **Treat forum posts as hypotheses, not facts:** Customer comments on Reddit represent psychological insights and angles to test, not absolute clinical truth [00:16:47].
- **The "Permanent Forces" lens uncovers root desires:** Surface-level complaints (such as hot flashes or hair thinning) roll up into deeper primal desires: regaining bodily control, restoring identity ("feeling like myself again"), and enjoying life rather than enduring it [00:22:57], [00:23:16].
- **Desire research changes the marketer:** The primary benefit of deep manual research is building genuine empathy with the target audience ("the research works more on you than you work on it"), which makes ad positioning authentic [00:27:52].
- **Research must feed market sophistication:** Finding a desire is only step one; if an audience has heard the phrase "take control" thousands of times, the marketer must introduce new mechanisms or underutilized benefit angles (e.g., hair growth or nail growth instead of generic skin health) [00:40:54], [00:42:24].

## Frameworks and models

### The Desire Hunting Sheet
A simple tracking layout used during market research [00:01:24]:
1. **Brand / Category:** Product being analyzed [00:01:30].
2. **Desires (Wants/Needs):** Underlying emotional or physical desires phrased as first-person statements (e.g., *"I want smoother and less dry skin"*, *"I want to be in control of my body"*) [00:01:38], [00:23:45].
3. **Customer Language Snippets:** Exact, unedited quotes from Reddit, reviews, or social media showing customer phrasing and complaints [00:01:47].
4. **Ad Ideas:** Creative angles, comparison concepts, hooks, or education plays derived directly from the snippets [00:01:58], [00:06:08].

### The AI Deep Research Workflow
A multi-step prompting protocol combining internal brand context with automated web research [00:28:20]:
1. **Prepare Context Documents:**
   - *How to Find Desires Guide:* Search protocols, keyword patterns, and trend criteria [00:29:15].
   - *Product Overview Document:* Technical vs. 6th-grade explanations of mechanisms, active ingredients, pricing, and outcomes [00:29:56].

4. **Digest and Synthesize:** Review AI output alongside manual notes to map pain points against permanent forces and spot unaddressed market complaints (e.g., clumping, GI distress, pill size) [00:34:25], [00:38:38].

## Numbers, benchmarks and rules of thumb

- **Manual research time:** Spend at least 25–30 minutes in the trenches on Reddit and YouTube before running or reading AI research reports [00:00:55], [00:27:12].
- **High-signal Reddit threads:** Look for threads with 100+ comments and look for individual comments with high relative upvotes (e.g., 20–35+ upvotes) as indicators of community resonance [00:03:00], [00:03:50], [00:29:15].
- **Google Trends rising desire indicator:** Search volume increasing 50%+ year-over-year, with related queries shifting from "what is" to "how to get" [00:29:15].
- **Menopause market facts:** Average transition duration is 7 years [00:25:09]; up to one-third of skin collagen is lost in the first 5 years post-menopause, followed by ~2% loss per year [00:38:55]; bone density drops up to 12% [00:34:10].
- **Absorption claim cited:** Marine collagen peptides absorb up to 30x better than standard bovine collagen [00:30:00].
- **Compliance rule:** Never make definitive medical claims that violate advertising guidelines; use compliant language (e.g., "might", "could") and focus on believability without deceptive assertions ("Don't be a piece of shit") [00:09:29].
