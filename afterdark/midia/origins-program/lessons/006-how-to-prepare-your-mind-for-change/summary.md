## Frameworks and models
### The Emotional Cycle of Change
A 5-stage progression mapped on a curve of emotional state (Optimism vs. Pessimism) over Time [00:03:05]:
1. **Stage 1: Uninformed Optimism** [00:02:00] – Initial excitement and high optimism driven by observing others' wins (e.g., watching online revenue screenshots) before encountering the operational workload [00:02:06].
2. **Stage 2: Informed Pessimism** [00:02:36] – A drop into pessimism as practical complexities emerge (store creation, product validation failures, creative asset production, media buying, CRO, offers) [00:02:22, 00:03:09].
3. **Stage 3: Valley of Despair** [00:03:27] – The lowest emotional point, characterized by volatile performance (e.g., store revenues dropping from $5,000/day to $500/day or zero) and the belief that the endeavor will never work [00:03:17, 00:03:27].
   - *Failure Path:* Quitting at this point and repeating stages 1–3 on a different business vehicle [00:04:04, visual notes 00:03:05].
   - *Growth Path:* Pushing through the difficulty to build real skills and personal change [00:05:20, visual notes 00:03:05].
4. **Stage 4: Informed Optimism** [00:05:20] – Ascending slope where things begin to work, conviction returns, and completed feedback loops drive progress [00:06:09, 00:06:17].
5. **Stage 5: Success & Fulfillment** [visual notes 00:03:05] – Peak where the business achieves repeatable, sustainable results matching the operator's expanded skill set [00:06:24, 00:07:32].
