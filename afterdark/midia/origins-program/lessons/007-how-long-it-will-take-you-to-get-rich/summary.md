## Overview
This lesson breaks down the realistic timeline required to build wealth in e-commerce using the 10,000-hour rule of deliberate practice. It is aimed at beginners and students with unrealistic expectations of getting rich quickly (within 30 to 60 days), resetting their commitment timeline to 3–4 years of dedicated skill acquisition.

## Key teachings
- Achieving world-class expertise in any field or skill requires approximately 10,000 hours of deliberate practice [00:00:27].
- While hitting $100,000/month in revenue (generating roughly $20,000 to $30,000/month in profit) does not require full world-class mastery, entrepreneurs will naturally desire more income to support their families and lifestyle [00:00:45].
- The more niche the focus (e.g., general marketing vs. e-commerce marketing vs. creative strategy), the more rapidly and effectively one develops world-class proficiency [00:01:47].
- Building durable wealth requires focusing on skill acquisition rather than seeking fast, lucky wins that cannot be sustained [00:07:28].
- If a student cannot mentally commit to putting in the necessary work over 3 to 4 years, they should leave the course rather than waste their time [00:08:26].
