#### **The 10,000-hour rule:**

**10,000 hours of deliberate practice is the key to achieving world-class expertise in any field**

**Working Every Single Day (not realistic)**

- 24 hours per day = **1.14 years**
- 12 hours per day =**2 years + 3 months**
- 8 hours per day = **3 years + 5 months**
- 4 hours per day = **6 years + 10 months**

**Working Every Day But One Day (1 Day Off Per Week)**

- 12 hours per day =**2 years + 8 months**
- 8 hours per day = **4 years**
- 4 hours per day = **8 years**

**Working Every Weekday (2 Days Off Per Week)**

- 12 hours per day =**3 years + 2 months**
- 8 hours per day = **4 years + 9 months**
- 4 hours per day = **9 years + 7 months**
