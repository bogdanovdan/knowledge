## Frameworks and models
### The First 30 Days Checklist
The instructor defines the foundational checklist required during the initial 30-day sprint [00:04:26]:
1. **Ad Creation Cadence:** Produce 1 concept per day (3–5 days a week).
2. **Offer Validation:** Find a strong offer that can convert.
3. **Feedback Loops:** Complete feedback loop reviews on every created ad.
4. **Competitor/Winning Ad Analysis:** Review and learn from proven winning ads to understand what successful creative looks like.
5. **Time Allocation:** Spend 90% of your time working outside of the Meta ad account.
6. **Technical Setup:** Ensure Meta ad account and Business Manager (BM) assets are properly configured.
7. **Mindset:** Do not give up.
