2. Sniper Method - Test within a strict environment to get closer to significant learnings as fast as possible.
[Image of a sniper rifle with bipod]
Now, unlike the Marksman method above, where you are essentially testing multiple angles at once,
The Sniper Method is ALL about testing different executions/styles of a specific angle.
Essentially, once you find an angle that you know is working, you then leverage the sniper method to go all in on that angle in order to create the best ads possible.
Pros: You will have a clearer understanding of what works, be more organized, learn what "works" faster, and have more confidence that you have validated specific angles.
Sniper Method REALLY crushes once you gain a direction from the Marksman Method so you can get the most out of angle.

## [00:16:55] The Shotgun Method and the #1 Mistake Brands Make

frame: 051_00-16-55.png (Drive: https://drive.google.com/file/d/15dMDiF4z-0otPkbDgV8EI1S-wZIrg6aJ/view?usp=drivesdk)

#3 - The Shotgun Method - Test a bunch of random things at once to see what sticks

[Image of elderly woman holding a shotgun]

The shotgun method is when you just launch a bunch of ads and hope something hits.

The right way to do this is through TikTok Shop content or our Insense product seeding/UGC strategy.

You get a bunch of content coming in from a bunch of different creators, and you don't really have time to go through and strategize every single one. So, you just launch all of the content in your ad account & hope that one of them hits.

And if they do, then you can process learnings to identify the angle, and use the Marksman Method or Sniper Method to refine it to create more winning ads

This is actually something that we did recently to scale a brand to 40K per day in spend.

Unfortunately, I cannot share that exact ad, but I promise you this strategy crushes.

The #1 Mistake Brands Make:

The WRONG way to do the shotgun strategy is WITH your creative strategy team.

DO NOT use shotguns in 2025 when it comes to creative strategy.

Don't just blast dozens of iterations or dozens of videos with small hook changes and just hope and pray that something hits.
