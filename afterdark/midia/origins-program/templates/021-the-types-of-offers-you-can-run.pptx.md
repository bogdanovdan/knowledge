⭐️ IMPORTANT NOTE #2

Prior to setting up any offer ALWAYS use the profit calculator to establish the new targets for ROAS & CPA so you know whether or not it is successful 
Evergreen Example:If your ROAS is currently a 2x and your break even ROAS is 1.43x (70% Gross profit) and your spending $100k per month, and you’re generating $200k total.
Your Gross Profit = $40,000 ($200k rev *0.7 gp = $140k gp - $100k spend)
New Offer Example:But if you launch a 30% offer Keeps you at a 2x ROAS but your breakeven is now 1.82x (55% Gross profit) and now your spending $150k per month, generating $300k total
Gross Profit = $15,000 ($300k rev *0.55 gp = $165k gp - $150k spend)Easy: https://www.easyamedia.com/profit-calculator
