### Step 1: Hypothesis

Why Do You Think This Did NOT Work? (Ranked most confident to least)

The visual and text, don’t really match up → So we’re saying “if you have an A/C” but we’re not really showing it

There is not strong visual language: How cold the AC is, or anything overly descriptive

Maybe the voiceover around “do you have AC” doesn’t really land because most people who can afford this product likely have AC/ The overall hook is pretty broad and not specific

Overall the video is not really exciting in the hook. It’s just showing a humidifier in a bedroom
