### Evolve Ad Formats

### Video Formats:

Voiceover with b-roll:

Voice-over footage/product shots (Real or AI)

Usually has low-volume background music that flows with the ad

Heavily edited with multiple clips (UGC,A-Roll,stock)

Example:
https://f.io/V_b1Th2b

Subtitles with b-roll:

Generated subtitles over footage/product shots (Taps into internal voice)

Usually, has background music flows with the ad

Heavily edited with multiple clips

Example:

https://f.io/PfaqXBS7

Slideshow Video w Text:

Static images with transitions

Heavy text overlay with movement

Text reveals

Example (has video clips, but mainly referring to the “Images”):
https://f.io/dGxDSUWH

Authentic UGC/Talking head only

Person speaking directly to camera (can be real or AI - use ai ay your own risk)

Casual, authentic feel

Usually single-take or minimal editing

Example:
https://www.instagram.com/p/DBs1mjjApc3/

Professional shot video:
CR8TIVE - Nurojit Video 1.mov

High production value

Professional lighting/equipment

Polished, branded look

Animation:

Animated characters/graphics

Can be 2D, 3D, or motion graphics

No real people/locations

### Image Formats:

Static graphic/design:

Colorfully designed graphics with text overlays (like Canva templates)

Has a visual of the product in it (can be without, but more for advanced marketers)

Think Bold headlines on branded backgrounds

Example:

Product photography + Headline

Clean, professional photos of the product

Can be on white/neutral backgrounds

Can be lifestyle shots as well

Example:

Infographic designs:

Visual charts, graphs, or step-by-step processes

Combines data/info with visual design

Example: "3 Steps to Better Sleep" with icons and text

Example:

Native-style image:

Designed to look like it’s native to the platform (organic)

Mimics organic social media posts (like Pubity style)

Often looks "unbranded" or casual

🚨IMPORTANT → DO NOT FAKE PRESS RELEASE ADS (This is false advertising)

Example:

Quote/Text-heavy image:

Mostly text with minimal design

Quote-style layouts

Text is the main focus, not visuals

Example:

Collage style:

Multiple images combined

Grid layouts or scattered arrangement

Shows multiple angles/uses

Example:

User-generated content (UGC) image:

Actual customer photos/selfies

Real people using your product

Authentic, unpolished look

Example:

Meme-style image:

Popular meme templates with custom text

Often uses recognizable meme formats

Example: Drake pointing meme with your product comparison
