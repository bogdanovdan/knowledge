### 2️⃣ Do’s & Dont’s

✅ DO:

Be authentic and speak from personal experience (whether as a working woman, mom, traveler, or fitness enthusiast).

Film in natural, well-lit settings (home, car, gym, office, outdoors).

Keep it fun, relatable, and unscripted.

Show real-life scenarios

❌ DON’T:

Over-script your video or use overly polished language—be natural!

Film in non-relevant settings (avoid overly staged or artificial environments).

Focus solely on the product without showcasing your real daily challenges.

### 3️⃣ Video Specs 📸

🎥 Format: Vertical (9:16) for TikTok/Reels.

⏳ Length: 30-60 sec.

🗣️ Vibe: Authentic, energetic, and relatable—your real-life moments that show off Sttoke’s unbeatable durability.

### 4️⃣ Hooks & Ideas

📌 Pick one or more ideas that fits your style best!

Concept #1: “Lost on the Roof” → “I literally left my cup on the roof and only noticed halfway through my drive—and it was still perfect!”

Concept: Show the hectic moment of being crazy busy–you putting your cup on the roof of your car while stuffing in your things–and you don’t realize til it flies off the roof when you drive off. Then reveal that your cup is still unscathed and still kept your coffee hot.

Concept #2: “Drop It Like It’s Hot” → “I drop my phone all the time, and my cup gets knocked around too—but it never cracks or leaks!”

Concept: Capture a series of accidental drops throughout your day, ending with a close-up of your cup remaining unscathed.

Concept #3: “On-the-Go, Still Hot” → “Between back-to-back meetings and constant errands, my coffee is still hot—my cup keeps up with my crazy day.”

Concept: Show snippets of your busy schedule where your cup endures it all while keeping your drink at the ideal temperature.
